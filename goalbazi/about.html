About Goalbazi

Football made easier to organize.

Goalbazi helps local athletes find matches, connect with players, discover arenas, build teams, run challenges, and follow leagues from one phone-first web app.

Find matches Explore arenas

For Athletes

Create your player profile, connect with friends, message athletes, join games, and build your football identity.

For Teams

Teams can manage members, challenge other squads, and appear in leagues with standings and stats.

For Arenas

Arena Partners can list grounds, add photos, describe facilities, and make their space easier to discover.

What Goalbazi Includes

  • Player profiles with ratings, positions, levels, and visual player cards.
  • Private messages, friend requests, team challenges, and notifications.
  • Arena search, match creation, league standings, and admin control tools.
  • Installable PWA experience designed mainly for mobile phones.
