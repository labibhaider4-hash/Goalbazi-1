Privacy Policy

Goalbazi collects only the information needed to run athlete profiles, arena discovery, bookings, messages, notifications, support, and admin safety controls.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as name, email, handle, location, position, skill level, and profile photo.
  • Booking details such as arena, slot time, UPI/UTR reference, amount, and booking status.
  • Messages, friend requests, ratings, reports, support requests, and app activity events.
  • Optional device notification tokens if you enable phone notifications.

How we use it

We use this data to provide the app, prevent misuse, support users, help arena partners manage bookings, and improve Goalbazi before launch.

Who can see it

Admins can access operational data for safety, support, and dispute handling. Arena partners can see booking details for their own arena only.

Your choices

You can request account help, correction, or deletion through the support page. You can also disable browser notifications from your phone settings.

Contact

For privacy requests, use the support page or contact the Goalbazi team.
