Refund & Cancellation Policy

This beta policy explains how booking and payment issues should be handled before automated gateway refunds are added.

Pending bookings

If a booking is pending and the arena partner has not confirmed it, the user should contact support with the booking details and UTR/payment proof.

Rejected bookings

If a paid booking is rejected, the user can raise a dispute through support. Admin will review the UTR, owner response, and booking status.

Confirmed bookings

Confirmed bookings are expected to be honored by the arena partner. Refunds after confirmation depend on the arena cancellation rules and admin review.

Owner payouts

Goalbazi may calculate owner payouts after deducting platform commission. Manual payouts should be recorded in the admin payout ledger.

Best future version

For full launch, Goalbazi should use Razorpay/Cashfree/PhonePe gateway payments with automated payment verification, refund tracking, and marketplace settlement.
